More on $5 International Roaming

If you are on a Business First or Government First plan, pay $5 extra per day (per service that is roaming) to use your plan inclusions in $5 Roaming destinations. Additional Roaming Data will be charged at $5/GB ($0.005/MB) if you use all your included Max Speed Data Allowance. Additional Roaming Data is shareable with other plans on the same account (excluding any plans with Unlimited Max Speed Data), expires at the end of your next billing period and can only be used in $5 Roaming destinations. You’ll only be charged for the days you make or receive a call, send a text or use your data overseas. The destinations where you can use $5 Roaming may vary, so check our current destinations above before you travel. You can use $5 Roaming for up to 90 days per calendar year.

Pay As You Go roaming

Pay As You Go roaming rates will apply:

  • If you’re travelling to a destination where $5 Roaming isn’t available.
  • If you have turned off $5 Roaming.

The rates for Business First plans’ Pay As You Go roaming are:

  • $1 per 60 seconds to make and receive calls.
  • $0.75 per standard text message sent (up to 160 characters), per recipient. No charge to receive SMS.
  • $0.75 per MMS message sent, per recipient. No charge to receive MMS.
  • $1 per MB data.

Pay As You Go roaming isn’t included in your plan and will incur extra charges on your bill.

In-Flight Roaming

With In-Flight Roaming, if you’re on an eligible mobile voice or mobile broadband plan, you can use your plan’s included data (excluding speed-capped data), calls and texts on selected flights with participating international airlines such as Cathay Pacific, Emirates, Etihad Airways, Lufthansa, Malaysia Airlines and Singapore Airlines for $5 extra a day when you use $5 Roaming.

Selected aircraft of these airlines are equipped with mobile in-flight equipment that has been specifically manufactured and tested so passengers can safely use mobile phones onboard.

How to access In-Flight Roaming:

Toggle on roaming: Prior to departure, ensure that roaming is active for the relevant service by contacting your account admin who can check via the self-service portal. (Note that roaming is “on” by default when your mobile service is activated.)

Switch on your phone: Once the plane has reached 20,000 ft and the pilot has activated In-Flight Roaming, switch on your device or toggle-off flight mode. You won’t access roaming if you keep your device turned off or on flight mode.

Enjoy In-Flight Roaming: Use your plan’s included data, calls and texts while on your flight. Excludes data capped at speeds of up to 2Mbps or 10Mbps. If you go over your included data allowance, we’ll automatically add 1GB for $5.

Additional information / FAQs

What are the participating airlines?

The participating airlines are Aer Lingus, Air Belgium, Asiana Airlines, Bangladesh Airlines, Cathay Pacific, Egyptair, Emirates, Etihad Airways, Eva Air, Airyways, Kuwait Airways, Lufthansa, Malaysia Airlines, SAS, Singapore Airlines, Swiss, Air Portugal, Turkish Airlines and Uzbekistan Airways.

These airlines may vary, so check our list before you travel.

How do I de-activate In-Flight Roaming?

If you’d prefer not to use In-Flight Roaming, keep your phone turned off or on Flight mode. You can also de-activate roaming by contacting your account admin.

What happens if I land in a $5 Roaming eligible destination?

If you used In-Flight Roaming during your flight and you landed within 24 hours, you can continue to use $5 Roaming without paying an additional $5. In-flight roaming and $5 Roaming are valid for 24 hours.

If you continue to use your included data, calls and texts after 24 hours from when you first used $5 Roaming, then you’ll be charged another $5.

What happens if I land in a Pay As You Go destination?

If you use data, calls and texts you’ll be charged at our Pay-As-You-Go rates.
